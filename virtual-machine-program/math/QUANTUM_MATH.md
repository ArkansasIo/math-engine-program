# Quantum Math Engine

The quantum extension provides mathematical primitives required by the virtual QCPU/QGPU.

## Core domains

- Complex arithmetic
- State vectors
- Density matrices
- Tensor products
- Hermitian/unitary operators
- Quantum gates
- Measurement
- Quantum Fourier transform
- Amplitude amplification
- Variational optimization
- Stabilizer and error-correction models

## Operation ID examples

QMATH-0001 — Complex addition
QMATH-0002 — Complex multiplication
QMATH-0010 — State-vector normalization
QMATH-0020 — Tensor product
QMATH-0030 — Unitary operator application
QMATH-0040 — Density-matrix evolution
QMATH-0050 — Measurement probability
QMATH-0060 — Quantum Fourier transform
QMATH-0070 — Amplitude amplification
QMATH-0080 — Variational objective

The quantum layer uses the existing Math Engine as its mathematical foundation rather than creating an unrelated second mathematics system.
